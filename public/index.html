Method
  • The wire countsModeling the boxes is the easy half. What most collections never model is what happens between them — transformers loading the input, cable capacitance rolling the top, one stage driven hot into the next.
  • Randomize, then judgeThe randomizer gets you somewhere you would not have typed. Your ears do the rest. Some units — by design — have none at all; a unit with one job does not need luck.
  • Mono-safe or it ships brokenWidth that collapses on a phone speaker is not width. Everything is checked in mono before it leaves.
