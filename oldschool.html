Ten processors, built as one chain, given away. No trial, no window, no countdown — the licence is free and it stays free.

Modelling the boxes is the easy half. What almost nobody models is the wire between them: transformers loading the input, cable capacitance rolling the top, one stage driven hot into the next. These ten were built together, so they load each other the way the originals actually did. Run them in series and the chain behaves like a chain.

They are free because the best argument for the rest of the catalogue is a plug-in you already use every day. Nothing here is a demo of something better. This is the thing.
