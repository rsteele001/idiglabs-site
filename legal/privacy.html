We never see your audio, your projects, or your files. The software does not upload, scan, or transmit anything you create. The only personal information we hold is what you gave us at checkout.

This policy explains what information Ron Steele, a sole proprietor trading as iDigLabs, collects, why, and what we do with it. It covers this website and our software.

1 · Who we are

Ron Steele, a sole proprietor trading as iDigLabs. For the purposes of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, we are the data controller. Contact: idiglabs@gmail.com.

2 · What we collect

At checkout. Your email address, name, and country. These are collected by Payhip (Payhip Ltd), our Merchant of Record, in order to process the transaction, calculate applicable tax, issue your licence key, and send your receipt and download link. We can see this information in our Payhip dashboard.

Payment details. We never see or store your card number. Payment information is handled entirely by Payhip and their payment processors.

On licence activation. When software validates a licence key, the key and a machine identifier are sent to Payhip's licensing service so the activation can be recorded against your licence. This is the only network request our software makes.

If you email us. Your email address and whatever you write in the message.

3 · What we do not collect

  • Your audio, projects, sessions, presets, or any file on your computer.
  • Usage analytics or telemetry from within the software. There is none.
  • Any record of which plug-ins you open, how often, or in which host.
  • Any advertising or tracking identifier.

4 · This website

The site is hosted by Netlify, Inc. Netlify records standard server request logs, which include IP addresses, for security and operational purposes.

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies on this site.

Two third parties serve content on our pages, and each will see your IP address as a technical necessity of delivering that content:

  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used on the site.
  • YouTube — hosts the demo video. The video is embedded behind a click-to-play thumbnail, so no YouTube script loads and no YouTube cookies are set unless you press play. If you press play, YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that playback.
  • Payhip — provides the checkout overlay when you click a download or purchase button.

5 · Why we hold it, and our lawful basis

  • Performing our contract with you — issuing your licence key, delivering the software, providing support, handling refunds.
  • Legal obligation — retaining transaction records for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Legitimate interests — preventing licence key abuse, and keeping our site secure and functioning.
  • Consent — if you opt in to product announcement emails. You may withdraw consent at any time.

6 · Email

We use your email address to send your licence key, receipt, and download link. That is a transactional message and you cannot opt out of it, because it is how you receive what you asked for.

We may occasionally email you about new products or important updates to software you hold a licence for. Every such message includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing never affects your licence.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your email address with anyone.

7 · Who else processes your data

  • Payhip (Payhip Ltd) — payments, tax, licence key issuance and validation.
  • Netlify, Inc. — website hosting.
  • Google LLC — font delivery; YouTube video hosting.

Each is an independent company with its own privacy policy. We do not share your information with anyone beyond what is necessary for these functions.

8 · International transfers

We are based in the United States. Our payment provider, Payhip, is based in the United Kingdom; our other service providers are US companies. If you are in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, or another region with data transfer restrictions, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States. Our providers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards for such transfers.

9 · How long we keep it

Transaction and licence records are retained for as long as your licence is active, and thereafter for the period required by tax and accounting law — generally seven years. Email correspondence is retained for up to three years. Marketing consent records are deleted on unsubscribe.

10 · Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is inaccurate
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Receive your information in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent to marketing at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with your data protection authority

To exercise any of these, email idiglabs@gmail.com. We will respond within thirty days. Note that deleting your transaction record may deactivate your licence key, since the record is what proves the licence is yours.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA, and we have not done so in the preceding twelve months. We do not use personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

11 · Children

Our software and store are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12 · Security

Transactions are encrypted in transit by Payhip. This site is served over HTTPS. We hold no payment details, so there is no card data with us to lose. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the amount of information we hold deliberately small.

13 · Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be noted on this page.
