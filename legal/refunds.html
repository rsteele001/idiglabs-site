1 · Scope

This policy applies to software sold by Ron Steele, a sole proprietor trading as iDigLabs. Purchases are processed by Payhip (Payhip Ltd), which acts as our Merchant of Record and issues refunds on our instruction.

2 · Try before you buy

Every paid product is available as a 30-day trial — the complete software, no features disabled, no watermarking. Download it, run it in your own sessions, on your own machine, in your own host. Nothing is hidden until after payment.

We would rather you took the trial than bought and refunded. It is less work for both of us and you get a better answer.

3 · Refund period

You may request a full refund within fourteen (14) days of the date of purchase. You do not need to give a reason. We will not ask you to prove the software is faulty, and we will not require you to work through troubleshooting steps before we agree to the refund.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Processing time depends on your bank or card issuer and is typically five to ten business days after we approve the request.

4 · How to request a refund

Email idiglabs@gmail.com with:

  • The email address used at checkout
  • The order number from your receipt, if you have it
  • The product name

You will receive a reply within two business days. You may also contact Payhip support directly using the link on your receipt.

5 · After a refund

When a refund is issued, the associated licence key is deactivated and your licence to use the software ends. You must remove all installed copies of the refunded software from your systems.

Audio you already created using the software remains entirely yours. A refund does not affect your rights in any work you have produced.

6 · Free software

Some of our software, including the Old School Series, is offered at no charge. Because no payment is taken, there is nothing to refund. If you no longer want free software, uninstall it — no notice to us is required. Free products carry no purchase obligation and do not enrol you in a subscription or trial that later converts to a paid plan.

7 · Bundles

Bundles are refunded as a single unit within the fourteen-day period. We do not issue partial refunds for individual products within a bundle.

8 · Requests after fourteen days

We are not obliged to refund after the fourteen-day period, but we would rather have a working relationship than a technically correct one. If the software is not functioning as described and the problem cannot be resolved, contact us and explain the situation. We will consider late requests on their merits.

9 · Statutory rights

This policy is offered in addition to, and does not limit, any statutory rights you have under the consumer protection law of your country of residence.

Customers in the United Kingdom and the European Union: you have a statutory right of withdrawal within fourteen days of purchase of digital content. Where you have consented to immediate delivery and acknowledged the loss of that right at checkout, this policy nonetheless grants you an equivalent fourteen-day refund window on the terms above.

10 · Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is wrong, contact us before disputing it with your bank. A chargeback costs us a fee and takes weeks to resolve. A refund request by email takes two days and costs you nothing. We would rather refund you.
